# 中文简介｜本体论在 AI 工程中的实践

大模型让 AI 更善于理解语言、生成内容和调用工具，但要让 AI 真正理解业务、可靠地执行任务，并在复杂系统中持续协作，我们还需要一套清晰、共享、可验证的业务语义体系。

**《本体论在 AI 工程中的实践》** 是「通往AI之桥」推出的技术实践系列，旨在探索本体（Ontology）如何从知识工程中的概念模型，发展为 AI 系统的重要工程资产。

本系列将从本体的基本概念出发，逐步深入本体建模、自动化构建工具、数据与系统映射、版本演进、质量验证，以及本体与 RAG、知识图谱、多智能体和 AI 软件工厂的融合。我们也将通过电商、软件工程、制造、金融等场景，讨论如何将抽象的业务知识转化为可复用、可治理、可验证的工程能力。

我们关注的不只是本体“是什么”，更是它**如何帮助 AI 建立一致的业务理解、连接真实数据与系统、约束智能体的行动，并支撑可持续演进的 AI 工程架构**。

从概念到模型，从模型到系统，从系统到真实应用——让本体成为连接业务世界与 AI 世界的一座桥。

# English Introduction | Ontology in AI Engineering: From Concepts to Practice

Large language models have made AI increasingly capable of understanding language, generating content, and using tools. But building AI systems that truly understand business domains, act reliably, and collaborate across complex systems requires something more: a shared, explicit, and verifiable semantic foundation.

**Ontology in AI Engineering: From Concepts to Practice** is a technical series by *The Bridge to AI* that explores how ontology can evolve from a knowledge-engineering concept into a practical engineering asset for modern AI systems.

Starting with foundational concepts, the series explores ontology modeling, automated ontology construction, data and system integration, versioning and evolution, quality validation, and the convergence of ontologies with RAG, knowledge graphs, multi-agent systems, and AI software factories. Through practical scenarios spanning e-commerce, software engineering, manufacturing, finance, and beyond, we examine how business knowledge can be transformed into reusable, governable, and verifiable engineering capabilities.

Our focus goes beyond what an ontology is. We explore **how it can help AI systems share a consistent understanding of business concepts, connect knowledge to real-world data and systems, constrain agent actions, and support continuously evolving AI architectures**.

From concepts to models, from models to systems, and from systems to real-world applications—building a bridge between the business world and the world of AI.
